# Kiro - Submission video crib sheet (filming, editing, YouTube)

**Not tested by Kiro** (Kiro cannot hold the phone). The shot list is in `docs/VIDEO-SCRIPT.md`.
**Checked on 22 Nov 2026** against https://kiroween.devpost.com/rules.

## The rules for the video (from the official rules)
- **Under 3 minutes.** Judges stop at 3:00. Aim for 2:50.
- Must show the project **working on the device it was built for**: your Android phone.
- **Publicly visible on YouTube**: choose **Public**, not Unlisted. Link on the form.
- **No third-party trademarks, copyrighted music or other material** without permission. See "Watch out" below.
- English.
- Judging (5 equal parts, 20% each): Potential impact, Technological implementation (Kiro use),
  Design, Innovation, **Presentation** ("does the video show it working end-to-end, who it is for,
  why it matters"). Best Demo is a separate $1,000 bonus, scored on Presentation.

## Watch out (trademarks and music)
- **Irn-Bru can:** a trademark. Do not show the can or its logo in the video.
  Film the Photo Treat with **shortbread, heather, a kilt or tartan, or bagpipes** instead.
  You can still *say* "Irn-Bru" in the voice-over.
- **Coke can refusal:** also a trademark. Use a plain mug or a banana for the "not a Scottish treat" shot.
- **Graveyard music** ("Flowers of the Forest"): traditional tune, old out-of-copyright recording (owner,
  23 Nov). Fine to use. Keep a note of where it came from in case YouTube's checker asks.
- **Background music:** use YouTube Audio Library (free to use) or none. No chart music.
- **Kiro, Cloudflare, Supabase, AWS names on screen:** fine, they are the tools you are describing.
- **Your email** on screen: use a throwaway or blur it. Never show `.env` files or the Supabase keys page.

## Before filming (once)
1. Phone: **Settings -> Display -> Screen timeout -> 10 minutes**.
2. Turn on **Do not disturb**, but allow Haggis-gotchi notifications (you want those on film).
3. Charge to 80%+. Clean the camera lens (for Photo Treat).
4. Make a **demo egg**: open `haggisgotchi.com/?demo=1&new=1`, tick **Demo wait**, tick the teen
   identity boxes, claim. (Demo eggs can use +1 day / Next stage / Skip to the end.)
5. Hidden demo menu: tap the Haggling's name **5 times quickly**.

## Screen-recording the Android phone
1. Swipe down twice from the top -> **Screen record** (if missing: tap the pencil and drag it in).
2. Choose **Record media sounds** (for the hatch film and chirps). **Show touches on screen**: on.
3. Tap **Start**. 3-2-1. Do the shot. Swipe down -> tap the red bar to stop.
4. The video lands in **Photos/Gallery -> Videos** (folder *Screen recordings*).
5. Film each shot as its **own short clip** (10-30 seconds). Easier to edit, easy to redo.

## Real-world footage (the phone in a hand)
Judges like seeing the real phone. Use a second phone or ask someone to film you:
- The phone in your hand, outside, in the rain or haar if Scotland obliges (weather section).
- Snapping shortbread for the Photo Treat.
- A notification arriving on the lock screen.
Landscape for these, held steady, daylight.

## Mac footage (Kiro and the mother page)
- **Cmd + Shift + 5** -> **Record selected portion** -> Options: microphone **None** (do the voice later).
- Kiro: open `haggis-gotchi-app`, make the font bigger (**Cmd +** twice), close side panels you do not need.
- Show: one spec (requirements -> design -> tasks), the steering files, a hook firing, an MCP call.
- Mother page at haggisgotchi.com/mother: the public scrapbook updating as you feed on the phone.

## Voice-over
- Record it last, over the edited picture. Quiet room, phone voice memo app is fine, 20 cm from your mouth.
- Read the script slowly. One take per section.

## Editing (free options)
- **iMovie** (on the Mac): drag clips in order, add the voice-over, add titles. File -> Share -> File, 1080p.
- **CapCut** (free) also works and adds captions automatically.
- Add **captions**: many judges watch muted.
- First 10 seconds must say what it is and who it is for.

## Upload to YouTube
1. youtube.com -> sign in -> **Create** (camera icon) -> **Upload videos**.
2. Title: `Haggis-gotchi - a Scottish folklore pet that lives on your phone (Kiroween 2026)`.
3. Description: one paragraph + `haggisgotchi.com` + the GitHub link + `#KiroweenHackathon26`.
4. **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids".
5. **Visibility: Public.** Publish.
6. Open the link in a private browser window to check it plays without signing in.
7. Paste the link into the Devpost form (see `docs/SUBMISSION-FORM-CRIB.md`).

**If YouTube flags copyrighted music:** remove the music in YouTube Studio -> Editor, or re-upload without it.
